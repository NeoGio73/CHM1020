  1. Drag a metal piece onto the balance pan and record its mass.
  2. Drag the same piece into the graduated cylinder. It already holds 26.2 mL of water.
  3. Open the cylinder close-up, read the bottom of the meniscus to the nearest 0.1 mL, and record the volume.
  4. Repeat with the other pieces, leaving the earlier ones in the cylinder.
